ART STYLE PROMPT

OVERVIEW
Create assets in a clean, stylized 2D isometric game-art style, like polished mobile or strategy-game assets (city builder, survival or island-village game). Everything should look friendly, readable at small sizes, and hand-crafted but tidy, with flat and soft-gradient shading rather than photorealism. Objects are isolated on a plain white or transparent background, and terrain is delivered as flat, seamless, tileable textures.

PROJECTION & COMPOSITION
- True isometric projection, 2:1 ratio (about 26.5° axes), identical across all assets so they fit together on one grid.
- Objects are viewed from above-front, with the top face, a left face and a right face visible.
- Light comes from the top-left: top faces are lightest, left faces mid-tone, right faces darkest.
- Objects are centered, cleanly cut out, with no background scenery, no cast ground shadows (at most a soft contact shadow or ripple), and no perspective distortion.
- Structures can be modular and repeating (docks, huts, tree clusters), built from simple geometric forms.

SHAPE LANGUAGE
- Simple, chunky, slightly exaggerated forms. Planks, posts and logs have rounded ends and soft corners.
- Low-poly faceted shapes for natural objects (rocks, tree canopies). Visible flat facets with gentle tonal differences, with no hard noise or texture photos.
- Thin dark-brown outlines or gaps separate parts (planks, thatch, logs). Outlines are subtle, not heavy black cartoon lines.
- Small details (knots, grain lines, ovals for nail heads, tufts, leaves) are drawn sparingly as simple vector-like marks.

COLOR PALETTE
- Wood: warm browns, with planks in three alternating tones (light tan #D9A76A, mid caramel #C8935A, darker #B8844F). Dark frame and posts in chocolate brown (#7A4F2A left face, #5A3A1A right face, #A87843 top cap).
- Water: bright aqua/sky blue (#62C3E3) with lighter cyan highlight lines (#9ADDF0) and soft mid-blue blobs.
- Sand: warm pale yellow-cream (#F5E3A8) with tiny darker orange speckles and pale highlight dots.
- Grass: soft sage/lime green (#9CC575) with slightly darker small grass-tuft marks and lighter clover-like patches.
- Foliage: lime-yellow highlights (#B5D956) fading into mid greens (#6FAE3E) for deciduous trees; deep teal-green (#2F7F4F to #3E9A5C) for conifers.
- Stone: cool light gray with lavender-gray shadow facets.
- Accents: coral/salmon red (#E8786E, #F29A82) for coral and fire orange (#F47B20 to #FFC54D) for flames; moss green (#6B8E3A) for decay.
- Overall mood is saturated but soft and pastel-leaning, with no harsh blacks and no neon.

SHADING & RENDERING
- Flat color fills with subtle gradients and soft facet shading. No realistic textures, no heavy ambient occlusion, no glossy reflections.
- Edges are crisp and clean for objects (vector-like), and soft and feathered for terrain transitions.
- Semi-transparent overlays (moss patches, algae, coral under water, ripples) are used for layering and weathering.

TERRAIN TEXTURES (tileable)
- Water: top-down-ish stylized caustic pattern of rounded blue blobs separated by pale interconnecting light lines, perfectly seamless.
- Sand: mottled cream-yellow with scattered tiny speckles, seamless.
- Grass: soft green with scattered tiny dark tufts and lighter blobs, seamless.
- Transition tiles (sand→water, sand→grass): organic noisy or feathered blend boundary with the texture of both sides continuing right up to the edge; water edge softly fades through sand, grass edge breaks up into patchy clumps.
- Variant tiles (e.g. water with a band of coral): semi-transparent, blurry-edged objects sitting under the water surface, with the water highlight lines drifting over them.

OBJECT / PROP RULES
- Trees: clusters of round deciduous trees (faceted lime-green canopies with a bright upper-left highlight, short brown trunks with flared roots) mixed with tiered conifers (scalloped layers, visible darker underside bands). Add small bushes, gray faceted rocks and tiny grass tufts scattered around the base.
- Buildings: round huts with thick layered thatched roofs (scalloped straw shingles in warm gold), a bundle of logs on the peak bound with rope, clay or sand-colored walls, wooden support poles, green cloth door flaps, small windows, clay pots and barrels, leafy plants at the base. Arrange them around a central campfire ring (stones, glowing flame, log benches) on a bare ground patch.
- Docks/piers: modular isometric wooden walkways on tall square posts, with posts topped by a lighter cap.

DECAY / PROGRESSION STATES (use for any structure that needs life stages)
1. Pristine: bright, clean, evenly colored wood, no marks.
2. Weathered: duller, desaturated wood, thin grain lines, moss patches, a few missing planks, small ripples around post bases, post bases tinted green, small grass tufts.
3. Ruined: heavy moss and algae, sagging or tilted sections, many missing planks, planks floating in the water, posts leaning, cracks visible, concentric ripple rings.
4. Collapsed: only fragments remain, with a few posts sticking out of the water, mostly overgrown with moss, floating debris everywhere, algae-covered, tilted posts, pale-blue ghost (translucent) planks, and a lot of ripples.
Each state keeps the same grid, scale, post positions and palette family, only shifting toward darker, greener, desaturated tones as decay increases.

WATER INTERACTION
- Every post or object touching water has 1-3 concentric pale teal ellipse ripple rings around its base (low-opacity, thin lines, isometric-flattened).
- Floating objects get larger fading ripple rings.
- The bottom of submerged posts shifts to moss-green.

DO
- Keep consistent scale, angle, line weight and palette across every asset.
- Keep backgrounds pure white or transparent for objects.
- Make tiles seamless and make assets look like they belong in the same game.

DON'T
- No photorealism, 3D renders with glossy materials, heavy black outlines, painterly brush texture, text, UI, characters, or perspective that breaks isometric consistency.
- No dramatic lighting, cast shadows across the ground, or dark, gritty, horror tones. The mood stays warm, cozy and inviting even when things are ruined.
